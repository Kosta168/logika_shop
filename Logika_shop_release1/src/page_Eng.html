Quick Overview

iPhone 16 Pro Max is the most expensive and technologically advanced iPhone of its generation. It combines the new A18 Pro processor, a large Super Retina XDR OLED display with adaptive ProMotion refresh rate up to 120Hz, an improved "Fusion" camera system, and excellent battery life for extended video and audio playback.
